## Scope

Close, in this order, the `unported!` calls of `ops/update_by/formula.rs` (U1 to U5 of
10.1: reads of a rolling window by position), U16 (`update_by` over vector or array
columns, `ops/update_by/values.rs`), `ops/select/arrays.rs` (6) and `materialize.rs` (1),
`ops/hierarchical/rollup.rs` (6) and `tree.rs` (3), and
`crates/oxhaven-server/src/table/ops/time_table.rs` (4): implement what Deephaven serves,
give Deephaven's error where it refuses, and leave, with an owner question, what Deephaven
answers in a way that depends on what a request cannot know, looks like a defect, or would
need a new allowed difference. The pending owner questions 1 to 4 of 10.1 are not touched.
