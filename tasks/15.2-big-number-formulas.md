## Scope

Owner's go-ahead (25 November: "Refusals, docs, Docker and HTTPS"). Serve formulas that compute with big numbers:
`BD + 1`, `BD * D`, `-BD`, `BI / 3`, casts between big numbers and primitives, the method calls Java's
formula language allows on them, and big numbers as arguments of the built-in functions that take them. Results
must equal Java's exactly, scale included (`BigDecimal#toString` text).

**Dependency.** A decimal type may come from a well-known crate under MIT or Apache-2.0 (for example `bigdecimal` or
`num-bigint`; check the whole new dependency tree with the licence check the `rules` job runs). Any other licence:
stop and report. Do not translate JDK source (`java.math`); implement from documented behaviour and fixtures recorded
from Java.

If a case cannot match Java exactly, keep it refused and list it; do not add PARITY.md entries.
