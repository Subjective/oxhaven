## Notes

- The decimal type of 15.2 moved to `oxhaven-column` (`decimal.rs`) for the engine to share.
- Served on every parent: sum, abs sum, avg, var and std of `BigDecimal` and `BigInteger`, with Java's
  running scale (`BigDecimalChunkedSumOperator`) and its zero-difference rule; checked on 42.9.
- Served: min, max, median, percentile, count distinct, distinct, unique and sorted first/last on static,
  add-only and blink parents, and on ticking parents while no group holds one number at two scales. Such a

  refreshing table with group-by columns that reaggregates big numbers (anything but first, last, group or
  formula of them) is unported, since Java builds those levels from the level below. Static rollups are
  served: a probe of every such aggregation of `BigDecimal` and `BigInteger`, every node expanded, gave
  the same client view on Oxhaven as on Java 42.9 (not kept as a scenario).
- Left for task 15.3b: the `update_by` operators, as-of stamps and range joins of big numbers (still
  refused).
