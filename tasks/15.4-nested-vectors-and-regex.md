## Scope

Owner's go-ahead (25 November). Two parts, in order; push after each.

1. **Vectors of vectors** (a `group_by` of a vector column, and what reads it): formulas that index or iterate
   them, `ungroup` of one level, and the aggregations and `update_by` operators that do not order them. Orderings of
   them stay refused (owner item 2.2).
2. **Regular expressions** in `matches` filters: lookaround, back references, possessive quantifiers and atomic
   groups through `fancy-regex` (MIT; check its dependency tree's licences). Translate Java's syntax and case
   folding to it; record Java's answers with `java.util.regex` on the reference's JDK; never translate JDK source.
   Whatever cannot match Java stays refused.
