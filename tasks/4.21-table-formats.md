## Scope

`Table.format_columns(formulas)`, `format_column_where(col, cond, formula)` and
`format_row_where(cond, formula)`: a format `Column = format` is an update view of a
hidden column, `<Column>__TABLE_STYLE_FORMAT` (a `long` style: `ColorUtil.toLong` of the
format), `__TABLE_NUMBER_FORMAT` for `Decimal(pattern)`, `__TABLE_DATE_FORMAT` for
`Date(pattern)`, and `__ROW__TABLE_STYLE_FORMAT` for `*`. The schema a client receives
links each to its column (`schema.rs` already did). The formula compiler has what a format
needs: the fields of `io.deephaven.gui.color.Color` as constants, colour names, and the
functions of `ColorUtilImpl` (`bg`, `fg`, `bgfg`, `bgfga`, `bgo`, `fgo`, `heatmap`,
`heatmapFg`, `colorRGB`, `colorHSL`, `toLong`, ...), with Java's choice of overload. A
colour is carried as its `long` style, only as an argument of those functions.
