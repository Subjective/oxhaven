## Scope

1. A family of the second generator that builds columns of `LocalDate`, `LocalTime`,
   `LocalDateTime`, `Duration`, `DayOfWeek`, `ZonedDateTime`, `Period` and `ZoneId` through
   formulas, with nulls and with one instant in several zones, on static and refreshing
   parents, and draws: sorts (one and several columns, descending), match and range filters
   with literal spellings, join keys (natural, exact, cross, as-of where Java allows the
   type), group keys, `select_distinct`, and the aggregations `min`, `max`, `first`,
   `last`, `count_distinct`, `unique`, `distinct`, `sorted_first`, `sorted_last`, `median`
   and percentile.
2. 1000 seeds of it against Java 42.9. Every difference is triaged as a refusal here of
   something Java computes (R), a Java defect (J) or a wrong answer here (D). D findings
   and cheap R findings are fixed; the others are written up below with a minimal scenario
   and a named entry in `fuzz_second.RESTRICTIONS`.
3. Known before the task started (`tasks/4.22-time-type-ops.md`): `min`, `max`,
   `count_distinct` and `unique` of a `Period`, sorted first or last by a `Period` on a
   refreshing parent, match and range filters on `LocalDateTime`.
4. All families of both generators again, 1000 seeds each.

Not in scope: `BigDecimal` and `BigInteger`; seed 308 of the family `all` (D5 of 4.20).

## Notes

Started from main at `963eb6fa`.

### What exists

- `oracle/harness/fuzz_times.py`: the generator `_Times` (variants `times`, family
  `time_types` in `fuzz.TIME_FAMILIES`), a generator of its own so that the pinned digests
  of the other two keep their scenarios (`tests/test_fuzz.py`, `test_fuzz_wide.py`,
  `test_fuzz_second.py`, `test_rules.py`: 104 passed). Its module comment says what it
  builds. It has no tests of its own yet and no pinned digest.
- `oracle/harness/rules.py`: columns of Arrow dates, times of day and durations
  (`LocalDate`, `LocalTime`, `Duration`) are compared exactly, as their integer counts,
  also inside arrays. This is a change to the comparison rules (`oracle-change` label).
  A `Period` is sent as an interval of months, whose cells pyarrow 26 cannot read (not
  even cast), so a table with a `Period` column is compared through a view without it.
- `oxhaven-expr`: `toLocalDateTime(Instant, ZoneId)` is computed (R1 below); it was the
  only way a formula could make a `LocalDateTime` column and was refused.

### Findings so far (Java 42.9 on port 10908, this server on 10909)

From the first 100 seeds: 66 clean, 34 with differences. Not yet triaged one by one; the
causes read from this server's log:

- **R1, fixed.** `toLocalDateTime(T, 'ET')` was refused ("unsupported in formulas").
- **R2.** A match filter on a `LocalDateTime` column is refused here
  (`MatchFilter.ColumnTypeConvertorFactory#getConvertor`), also `M in null` and
  `M not in null`, which the generator still draws; Java computes. Known from 4.22.
- **R3, R4, R5.** Known from 4.22 and stepped around from the start, not observed again:
  the ordering aggregations of a `Period`, sorted first or last by a `Period` on a
  refreshing parent, a `Period` compared with a period literal.
- **R6, new.** An as-of join on a column of any of these types is refused here by name
  (`AsOfJoinHelper#asOfJoin: an as-of join on a column of type LocalTime`, and the same for
  `LocalDate`, `LocalDateTime`, `DayOfWeek`, `Duration`, `ZonedDateTime`); Java computes
  them in at least 14 of the 100 seeds. For `Period` and `ZoneId` it is not known yet
  whether Java refuses.
- Not looked at: 16 seeds where a `view` fails here and not in Java (most are the view
  of a table this server refused, a few may be `hourOfDay(Z, true)` or something else);
  8 seeds where a `where` is refused here and 3 where its status differs; 3 seeds each of
  `agg_by` and `sort`, 2 of `join`, 1 each of `natural_join` and `select_distinct` (may
  all be downstream of a refused parent).
- Formulas refused here that Java computes, found while choosing how to show a value
  (not drawn by the generator): `toLocalDateTime(ZonedDateTime)`, `toLocalDate(Z)`,
  `toLocalTime(Z)`, `Z.getZone()`, `Z.toString()`, `P.toString()`, `formatDateTime(Z)`,
  `formatDate(D)`, `toZonedDateTime(D, L, zone)`, `D.atTime(L)`, `parseLocalDateTime`.
