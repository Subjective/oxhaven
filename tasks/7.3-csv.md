## Scope

**Needs its own design first.** This file marks the area so the plan has no blank; the architect of M7 replaces it with sized tasks. The first deliverable is a short design document, as `docs/CONSOLE.md` is for the console.

`read_csv` and `write_csv`. The work is in the type inference: Java's reader decides
column types from the data with documented rules (integer widths, doubles, booleans,
date-times, strings) and those decisions are user-visible. Decide whether to reimplement
the rules over a Rust CSV parser or to bind something existing, and how to record Java's
inference on a corpus of awkward files.
