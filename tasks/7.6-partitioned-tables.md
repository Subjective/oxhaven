## Scope

The design is `docs/PARTITIONED.md`. This task built its first slice; the rest is the tasks
below, which the plan takes up as files of their own.

Done here:

- The column type `ColumnType::Table` in the contracts, and a column of tables through the
  operators that move cells, to clients (as Java's strings) and in `meta_table`.
- `partition_by` of static and ticking tables, with and without the key columns, by any
  number of columns; the partition aggregation in `agg_by`; `partitioned_agg_by`.
- Constituents that follow their group, `merge` that follows the constituents,
  `filter` and `sort` of a partitioned table, one constituent by its key.
- `PartitionedTableService`: `PartitionBy`, `Merge`, `GetTable` in both modes. The object
  type `PartitionedTable` in `ListFields`, `FetchObject` and `MessageStream`.
- Python: `PartitionedTable` and `Table.partition_by`, `Table.partitioned_agg_by`,
  `agg.partition`; `transform` of a partitioned table that is not refreshing.
