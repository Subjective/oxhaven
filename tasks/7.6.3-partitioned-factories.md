## Scope

- `PartitionedTable.from_partitioned_table`, with every argument and with none
  (`PartitionedTableFactory#of`), and `from_constituent_tables`
  (`PartitionedTableFactory#ofTables`), with Java's checks and texts.
- `partitioned_transform` of a partitioned table whose keys are not unique: the `join` of
  the two underlying tables.
- A copied cell of a column of tables (`select`, `snapshot`, a static `update`) keeps its
  table, as Java's does.

Then, as far as context allows (otherwise task 7.6.4 stays as sized in
`tasks/7.6-partitioned-tables.md`): `get_constituent` with a key value Java converts;
match filters and `where_in` on a column of tables; a column of tables in a formula.
